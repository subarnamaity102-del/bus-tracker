About Bus Tracker Project

A simple, handcrafted public transport tracking website built with modern HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.

Our Mission

Bus Tracker was created to provide a simple, clean, and reliable interface for commuters to track city bus routes, estimated arrival times, and live bus statuses.

Whether you are a daily commuter heading to work, a college student reaching campus, or a tourist exploring the city, our application eliminates transit uncertainty by providing live updates right at your fingertips.
